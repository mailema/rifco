About RIFCO

A Home for Football Coaches

RIFCO is a football coaches organization dedicated to raising the standard of the game at the grassroots level — supporting coaches, organizing teams and leagues, developing players, and administering responsible player transfers.

  • Supporting and representing football coaches
  • Organizing structured teams and leagues
  • Player development and registration
  • Responsible, auditable player transfers
  • Strengthening football administration
