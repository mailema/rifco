About RIFCO

The Ringim Football Coaches Organization exists to support football coaches, organize teams and leagues, develop players, and administer responsible player transfers across the region.

Our Mission

RIFCO strengthens football administration by giving coaches, teams, and leagues a shared, professional structure to operate within — from registration through to player movement.

  • Supporting football coaches
  • Organizing teams and leagues
  • Player development
  • Responsible player transfers
  • Strengthening football administration
